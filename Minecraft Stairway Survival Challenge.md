# Minecraft Stairway Survival Challenge## The Challenge

You have been trapped in a 3×3 chunk of land! In survival mode, you will have to dig, fight, and grow your way up and out to the top of your chunk by creating a giant staircase into the sky!

When someone reaches the top of the sky, the world will start falling apart, and the last player standing wins!

## The Gameplay

* You are assigned to and trapped in a 3×3 chunk of land in Minecraft within a much larger chunk of land that makes up the gameplay area.
* The game is split into two phases: The building phase, and the survival phase.
* During the building phase, you may do whatever you like inside of your designated area.
* Venturing a single block outside of this area will afflict you with a non-fatal (but very painful) concoction of status effects.
* Venturing any further outside of this area will afflict you with Wither V permanently.
* When you die during the build phase, so long as you have a bed, you will be able to respawn in your chunk, but with a random penalty applied.
* The first person to get to the top of the world will activate the survival phase.
* Once the survival phase is activated, you will be free to venture out of your chunks, but the world will start falling apart level-by-level every few minutes starting from the void and eventually reaching the top of the world.
* Every time a level of the world is removed, a random effect will be applied to you or the environment.
* The last man standing will win the challenge, and Mr Beast will donate 100 Million Lamborghinis to sub-saharan Africa

## The Rules

* Leaving the overworld for another realm like the nether will result in disqualification.
* Breaking your bed will remove your respawn point.
* Ender pearls are the only legal way for you to leave your chunk. Any other means of leaving the chunk during the building phase will result in disqualification.
* Leaving the gameplay area by any means will result in disqualification.
