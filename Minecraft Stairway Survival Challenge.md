# Minecraft Stairway Survival Challenge

## The Challenge

You have been trapped in a 3×3 plot of land! In survival mode, you will have to dig, fight, and grow your way up and out to the top of your plot by creating a giant staircase into the sky!

When someone reaches Y=319, the world will start falling apart, and the last player standing wins!

## The Gameplay

* You are assigned to and trapped in a 3×3 plot of land within a much larger gameplay area.
* The game is split into two phases: The building phase, and the survival phase.
* During the building phase, you may do whatever you like inside of your designated plot.
* Venturing a single block outside of a plot will afflict you with a non-fatal (but very painful) concoction of status effects.
* Venturing any further outside of a plot will afflict you with Wither V permanently.
* When you die during the build phase, so long as you have a bed, you will be able to respawn in your plot, but with a random penalty applied.
* The first person to reach Y=319 will activate the survival phase.
* Once the survival phase is activated, you will be free to leave your plot, but the world will start falling apart: every minute, two layers are removed, starting from the void and eventually reaching the top of the world.
* Every minute, a random effect will be applied to you or the environment.
* The last man standing will win the challenge, and Mr Beast will donate 100 Million Lamborghinis to sub-saharan Africa

## The Rules

* Leaving the overworld for another realm like the nether will result in disqualification.
* Breaking your bed will remove your respawn point.
* Ender pearls are the only way to move between plots without penalty.
* Leaving the gameplay area by any means will result in disqualification.
